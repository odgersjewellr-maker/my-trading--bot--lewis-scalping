Does London's direction carry into New York?

From the London open the market picks a way. The trader's assumption is that New York keeps pushing it. Across 3,329 weekday sessions of minute data, that assumption is barely better than a coin flip — and in the first two hours after the NY open it slightly reverses. Here's the real shape, and the one asymmetry worth trading.

The shape: fade early, reassert late

Probability that price is still going London's way, measured hour by hour after the New York open (2019+). It dips below the coin-flip line right after the open, then the original direction slowly comes back into the US afternoon.

The one asymmetry: buy weakness, don't sell strength

Split the NY-open reaction by which way London went (2019+, held 2h). Fading a down-London — buying the dip — pays. Fading an up-London — shorting the rip — doesn't. It's BTC's long bias, and it's stable across session definitions.

Why ">50%" isn't free money

The fade wins slightly more often, but each winner is smaller than each loser (+0.54% vs −0.67% at +2h) — you clip small gains until a trend day runs you over. Net profit factor sits at ~0.9–1.2 gross, and its sign flips between session definitions. After ~0.05–0.10% round-trip cost, the naive "follow London" and "fade the open" trades are both break-even to negative. This is a filter, not a trigger.

How to trade it

Highest-confidence idea first. Everything here is context to stack with your own setups — sized off a typical NY session (median range 2.6%, median net move ~1.0%).

◆ Do

  • 1Long the NY-open dip after a down London — the one robustly positive setup (~54%). Best while price is above its 20-day trend.
  • 2Expect the open trap. To trade London's way, wait out the first 1–2h and enter the pullback; it reasserts into the US afternoon (~53%).
  • 3Use it as confluence. Best continuation = London dir aligned with the 20-day trend and a ≥0.5% London push (~53–55%).
  • 4Anchor to local clocks. London 08:00 / NY 09:30 local — the effect shifts an hour with daylight-saving.

✕ Don't

  • ✕Chase the NY open in London's direction. Into the first 1–2h that's a below-50% bet.
  • ✕Short a strong up-London into New York. Negative expectancy (PF 0.75–0.90).
  • ✕Treat it as mechanical. The gross edge is ~half a round-trip fee — trade only the filtered setups, prefer maker fills.
  • ✕Assume it's constant. Yearly continuation swings 45%→57%. A soft, drifting tendency.

Method & caveats. BTC/USD, Bitstamp 1-minute candles, weekdays only. Continuation is measured on hourly closes (no intrabar stop/target path); P&L is gross of fees; one instrument/venue.
