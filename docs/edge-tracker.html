Live-readiness gates (every edge must clear all of them)

gatewhat it means
1 · Out-of-samplePassed a holdout, walk-forward, or unchanged run on an unseen asset or window. In-sample results don't count.
2 · Forward paper60–90 days (or the edge's own trade-count gate) on the exact config that was validated, on a live feed.
3 · ExecutionVenue adapter tested, real fees and slippage measured, and paper results reconciled against the backtest.
4 · GuardrailsSpend cap, daily trade cap, drawdown halt and kill criteria set before the first live order.
5 · CorrelationSized alongside every other live edge; the correlated crypto-trend cluster gets one combined allocation.
